A little encouragement

First time speaking at a school committee meeting?

It's less intimidating than it sounds. Anyone can speak during public comment. Here's the quick version, and our sample comments page has more.

  1. Two to three minutesOne clear point lands better than five rushed ones.
  2. Start with your storyWhat you've seen with your own kids is what committee members remember.
  3. Read from the pageWritten remarks work beautifully, in person or on Zoom.
  4. Close with an ask, and thanksStay constructive. Committee members are volunteers carrying a heavy load.

Fill out a short form when you arrive. Arriving late is fine. And you can always simply attend: a full room speaks, even when you don't.
