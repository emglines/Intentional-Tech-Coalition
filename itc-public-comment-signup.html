Take Action

Sign up to speak at a school committee meeting

Three speaker slots per meeting. Expertise is a bonus, not a requirement — just bring your voice. Speakers have 2–3 minutes and are welcome to read from a printed sheet. You can attend in person or via Zoom.

Setup needed before this page goes live. This page stores sign-up data in Firebase. To activate it:
  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com, create a project, and add a Realtime Database (choose "Start in test mode").
  2. Copy your database URL — it looks like https://your-project-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com
  3. Open this file and find the line near the top of the <script> that says const FIREBASE_URL = ""; and paste your URL between the quotes.
  4. Save and push the file. The warning disappears automatically once the URL is set.
