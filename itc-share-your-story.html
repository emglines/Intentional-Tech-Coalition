How we use your story

We read every one

Every story goes to a member of our leadership team, and only our team sees what you send.

Always anonymous

If you say yes, we may share short excerpts on our website or in our advocacy. We never include names, and we remove details that could identify a family, child, teacher, or school.

A small request

Please don't name teachers, staff, or other children. Describe the situation instead. Concord is a small town, and this keeps everyone's privacy intact.
