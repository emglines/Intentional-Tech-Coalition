Concord's data · Updated for 2026

What Concord's data show

Every two years, the Emerson Youth Risk Behavior Survey reaches nearly every student in grades 6, 8, and 9 through 12, in Concord-Carlisle and other area districts. The 2026 results are in, and set beside 2024 they tell a clear story: Concord families are changing how technology works at home, and students' answers show it on almost every home measure. The school day has not moved the same way. Below are the survey's numbers side by side, and what you can do about the school day.

Where phones stay off from bell to bell, the gains are biggest

Concord's schools have two different phone rules, and between 2024 and 2026 the two groups' results moved very differently.

Ellen Garrison Building at Concord Middle School

Off and in backpacks, bell to bell

Phones are turned off and left in students' backpacks for the whole school day. Elementary schools follow the same rule.

Concord-Carlisle High School

Put away during class only

Phones only required to be put away during class. They may be used during lunch, passing time, and all other time outside class.

What we're asking

Our 2026-27 school priorities
Safety Is it safe for our children?
  • A public inventory of every student-facing tool and the data it collects
  • Generative AI off by default until reviewed
  • School devices that reach approved sites only
 Efficacy Does it help students learn?
  • Evidence shared before any student-facing tool is adopted
  • Proof from independent research, not the company selling it
  • Fewer, better tools by June 2027
 Alignment Does it reflect our district's own goals?
  • Real partnership with parents on the District Technology Committee
  • Technology decisions that support the goal to cut student stress in half
  • A parent app that shows screen time on school devices
And bell to bell at every school. Bring Concord-Carlisle High School in line with the elementary schools and the Ellen Garrison Building at Concord Middle School: phones off and away from first bell to last, with secure storage.
