Ways to help

Volunteer roles for 2026-27

These roles will help us bring our 2026-27 priorities to life. Many hands make light work, so anyone who takes on a role is welcome to recruit others to help wherever they see fit. Our leadership team is here to support every effort along the way.

Filter by time commitment

Public Comment

2 hours every two weeks

Fills all three public comment slots at each School Committee meeting.

  • A good fit if you are organized, comfortable following up with people, and like helping others feel ready to speak
  • Shares ITC's public comment tips
  • Supports speakers as they prepare
I'm interested

School Correspondence

2 hours a month

Sends one to two coalition emails a month to the School Committee and central office.

  • A good fit if you write clearly and concisely and are comfortable with a constructive, cooperative tone
  • Updates and suggestions on behalf of ITC
I'm interested

Communications

2 to 4 hours a month

Puts together the monthly ITC newsletter and sends blurbs to school PTG newsletters.

  • A good fit if you enjoy writing and editing and like pulling information together into a polished format
  • Our point of contact for sharing information with the schools
I'm interested

Outreach

1 to 2 hours a week

Finds local events and other ways to raise ITC's visibility in town.

  • A good fit if you keep up with what's happening around town and enjoy connecting people with opportunities
  • Arranges flyers and volunteers for events
  • No need to attend every event yourself
I'm interested

Social Media

1 to 2 hours a week

Keeps ITC present on social media and in local parent groups.

  • A good fit if you are comfortable posting online and enjoy keeping a steady, friendly presence in local conversations
  • Shares ITC news, events, and resources
  • Watches local parent groups for chances to be helpful
I'm interested

Press

1 to 2 hours a month

Looks for opportunities to share ITC's story in the press and responds to incoming press inquiries.

  • A good fit if you enjoy persuasive writing and are comfortable reaching out to a local newspaper
  • Writes a few letters to the editor about our work
I'm interested

Business Partnerships

1 hour a week

Invites local businesses to set aside a device-free space or time.

  • A good fit if you like talking with local business owners and enjoy building relationships in the community
  • Shares ITC materials on the benefits
  • Keeps our partner business list current
I'm interested

Community Coffee

1 to 2 hours a month, not including attending

Plans five informal community coffees through the end of the school year.

  • A good fit if you enjoy planning and hosting and like bringing people together in a relaxed setting
  • Schedules one or two leadership team members per coffee, on a rotation
I'm interested

Research

5 to 10 hours to start, then 1 to 2 hours a month

Gathers public information on the district's EdTech contracts, programs, and spending.

  • A good fit if you are comfortable reading budgets and public documents and enjoy organizing information
  • Combines it with other data ITC collects
